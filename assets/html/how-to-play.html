HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys

Interact: SPACE

OBJECTIVE

You are a teenage single parent balancing school, work, and raising a baby. Your goal is to survive 12 months without dropping out of school or neglecting your baby's health.

GAME OVER CONDITIONS

The game ends if Baby Health reaches 0% or if you fail to balance your life by the end of the year.
